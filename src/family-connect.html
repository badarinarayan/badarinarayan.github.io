Family Connect

SSIR brought our two worlds together for a day — and it turned out to be more meaningful than any of us expected.

SSIR's "Family Connect" event gave family members a chance to visit and experience our workplace firsthand.

To be honest, the thought of coming to the office on a weekend wasn't very appealing — especially with the long commute, traffic, and forecasts of heavy rain. But once we got there, it turned out to be a truly heartwarming experience.

It was wonderful to see family members taking pride in the place where we spend a significant part of our lives — often more waking hours than we spend with them. Seeing our work environment through their eyes was a special feeling.

The event had several engaging activities for children, including face painting and origami workshops, which brought plenty of smiles. My son was especially thrilled to win a Samsung cap in the lucky spin-the-wheel game. Daughter too was delighted to get tattoo painted on her hand and doing Origami art.

One of the most enjoyable parts of the day was seeing colleagues in a completely different light — away from meetings, deadlines, and work discussions, and instead as parents, spouses, and family members. 😊

Overall, it was a thoughtfully organized and memorable event. Kudos to SSIR for creating an opportunity that helped bridge our professional and personal worlds. 👏
